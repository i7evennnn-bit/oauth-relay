Social Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Social Studio is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner connect their own social media accounts, including TikTok, and publish or schedule their own videos.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives basic profile information (such as display name) and permission to publish videos to that same account. The app only posts videos that the owner chooses or queues.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the app, and they are not shared with anyone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to the platform the owner selected, in order to publish them.

Deleting your data

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time inside the app, which deletes the stored token, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions.
